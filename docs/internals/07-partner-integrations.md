# Partner & Course Integrations (Skill-Gap Ecosystem)

> **Strategic Focus:** Integration with premier Indonesian developer & professional training platforms (such as **Dicoding** and **Kartu Prakerja**) for automated course recommendations in the AI skill gap engine.

## Overview

The KerjaCerdas Skill-Gap analyzer maps job seeker competencies against job market requirements. When skill deficiencies are identified, the system connects seekers with targeted curriculum tracks.

## Integration Architecture

1. **Course Ingestion:**
   - Standardized `Course` catalog schema (`backend/app/db/models.py`) storing provider, skills taught, duration, Prakerja subsidy eligibility, and direct deep-link URLs.
   - Provider tags: `Dicoding`, `Coursera ID`, `Prakerja`, `MySkill`, etc.

2. **Skill-to-Curriculum Matching:**
   - Heuristic and embedding-assisted matching between `missing_skills` and `skills_taught`.
   - Structured payload generated by `backend/app/services/matching/matcher.py` surfacing actionable course recommendations with cost estimates in IDR.

3. **Partnership Inquiries:**
   - Enterprise & training partners can submit onboarding and partnership inquiries via `/api/v1/inquiries`.
